Summation
ERP.

A modular operating system that puts sales, inventory, production, purchasing, finance, and people operations into one clear workspace.

CategoryEnterprise platform
PlatformResponsive web app
FocusOperational clarity
StackReact · Node.js · PostgreSQL · AWS

One practical home for complex operations.

Summation ERP reduces the friction of running a growing business by giving teams a shared, current view of what needs attention. The dashboard is built around useful signals — stock health, purchase orders, pipeline, quality control, and invoices — so teams can act without hunting through separate systems.

  • Inventory & procurementTrack components, materials, vendors, purchase orders, and incoming stock with a clear audit trail.
  • Manufacturing workflowsBring job cards, sub-assemblies, quality control, and fulfilment into a consistent production process.
  • Decision-ready dashboardSurface the status, exceptions, and commercial numbers that need a manager’s attention today.
