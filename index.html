About

Tech expert, entrepreneur,
builder.

I'm Nikhilesh Wani — a tech expert and entrepreneur based in Pune, Maharashtra. I work at the intersection of technology and business operations, turning ideas into products people actually use.

I founded Byteseal — a biometric hardware credential vault — out of a passion for building products that solve real problems. That journey led to two granted Indian patents and years of hands-on experience shipping hardware and software from zero to one.

I also take on select consulting engagements, helping teams design, build, and ship web and mobile products. With 8+ years of hands-on engineering, I bring both the technical depth to solve hard problems and the product judgment to know which problems matter.
