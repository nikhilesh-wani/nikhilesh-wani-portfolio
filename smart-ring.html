Smart Ring
Companion.

A connected-health mobile experience that turns a stream of wearable data into an interface people can understand at a glance.

CategoryMobile health
PlatformiOS & Android
FocusConnected-device UX
StackFlutter · Dart · BLE

Health data, with a human point of view.

The product brings a broad set of ring signals — sleep stages, heart rate, HRV, activity, blood oxygen, and readiness — into a single elegant companion app. The design prioritizes the most relevant signal for the moment while still offering deeper detail for people who want it.

  • Rest & recoveryDaily sleep timelines, quality scores, and weekly patterns help people understand how they are recovering.
  • Activity at a glanceProgress rings, movement trends, and workout summaries create a lightweight daily check-in.
  • Device controlBattery, connection state, settings, and firmware updates live in a calm, dedicated device space.
