Support
Desk.

A unified customer-service workspace that helps teams keep conversations moving, communicate context, and resolve issues without the tab switching.

CategoryService operations
PlatformResponsive web app
FocusTicket & conversation flow
StackReact · Node.js · WebSockets · Supabase

Support that stays in context.

Support Desk brings the inbox, ticket history, customer data, assignments, internal escalations, and replies into one focused interface. The experience makes it easy for a support agent to see what has happened, choose the right next action, and hand off work to the right specialist when needed.

  • One continuous conversationCustomer messages, status, and previous touchpoints remain in view instead of being scattered across tools.
  • Clear ownershipAssignments, contact actions, and escalation controls make the path to resolution visible to the whole team.
  • AI-ready assistanceBuilt to support AI responses and agent takeover, so automation can help without losing a human handover.
