CIRP
Marketplace.

A professional-resolution marketplace that makes complex case information, participants, documents, and bidding activity easier to coordinate.

CategoryProfessional marketplace
PlatformResponsive web app
FocusStructured case workflow
StackWeb platform · Documents · Workflow UX

Bring order to high-stakes work.

CIRP Marketplace gives professionals a structured environment to list cases, invite participants, verify details, review documents, coordinate finance modes, and manage bids. The product turns a multi-party process into one clear, traceable workflow without reducing the nuance of the work.

  • Case information in one placeKey details, summaries, documents, and dates are organized around the listing rather than scattered across communications.
  • Collaborative by designInvite committee members and keep their participation, acceptance, and inputs visible to the right people.
  • Professional controlVerification and clearly defined listing states help establish a trustworthy workspace for sensitive workflows.
